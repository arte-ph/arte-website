Got Questions?

FAQs

Is there a fee to join Arte?
Arte does not take any cuts or commissions from your rates for now, so you receive your full service fee. We charge clients a small admin fee (PHP 250.00) for coordination and booking support.

As we continue to grow and launch our platform, this may evolve. If anything changes, we’ll make sure to inform you and take our partner artists’ feedback into consideration.
When do I get paid?
Clients send their downpayment to Arte. We hold it and release it to you after the service. This protects both you and the client, and you’ll still receive the full downpayment even if the client doesn’t show up.

If you prefer a different setup, just let us know. We’re open to adjusting and finding a setup that works best for you.
Can I decline bookings?
Yes, you are free to accept or decline any booking based on your availability and preferences.
Do I need to be exclusive with Arte?
You don't need to be exclusive with Arte. You can continue accepting bookings from other platforms or personal clients.
Can I still apply if I’m outside your service areas?
Arte is open to artists in Metro Manila, CALABARZON, and nearby areas. If you’re based elsewhere, you can still apply as we may expand over time.
