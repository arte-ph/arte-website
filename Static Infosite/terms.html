01

What Arte Does

Arte connects clients with verified, independent beauty professionals — including hair and makeup artists (HMUAs), nail technicians, and lash technicians. We handle coordination, booking management, and client support so that both sides of every appointment have a smooth and stress-free experience.

Specifically, Arte provides:

  • A curated network of verified beauty professionals across Metro Manila and CALABARZON.
  • Booking coordination and inquiry management between clients and artists.
  • Secure downpayment handling to confirm and protect bookings.
  • Dedicated support for concerns such as no-shows, sudden cancellations, and disputes.
  • Matching assistance — if your preferred artist isn't available, we'll help find one who fits your needs, date, and location.
Arte facilitates the connection between you and your artist. Once a booking is confirmed, clients and professionals are encouraged to communicate directly to finalize details.
02

Booking Process

Here's how a typical booking works through Arte:

  • You submit an inquiry with your preferred service, date, time, and location.
  • Arte presents your request to matched artists and confirms availability.
  • Once a match is found, you'll receive an invoice detailing the service breakdown, downpayment amount, and applicable Arte admin fee.
  • Your slot is secured once the downpayment is received by Arte.
  • After confirmation, the artist will reach out directly to finalize details (e.g., look preferences, exact location, skin or nail prep).

Booking through Arte means you agree to the terms set by both Arte and the matched artist, including any service-specific policies communicated before confirmation.

03

Payments & Arte Admin Fee

Downpayment

To secure your booking, clients are required to pay a downpayment to Arte. The downpayment amount depends on the artist's individual policy and will be clearly stated in your invoice before you confirm.

The remaining balance is paid directly to the artist after the service is completed. Payment method for the balance is agreed upon between the client and the artist.

Payment Deadline

Downpayments must be completed by 11:59 PM on the day after the invoice is sent. Failure to pay within this window may result in your booking slot being released.

Non-Refundable Deposit

All downpayments are non-refundable. This protects the artist's time, which is reserved exclusively for you once your booking is confirmed.

Booking Policies

Each artist on Arte may have their own policies around cancellations, rescheduling, late arrivals, no-shows, and early call times. These will always be communicated to you before your booking is finalized.

By completing your booking, you agree to both Arte's general terms and the specific policies of your chosen artist.

Cancellation

Cancellation fees and conditions vary per artist. Arte will clearly communicate the applicable cancellation policy as part of the booking process. Since downpayments are non-refundable, clients are encouraged to double-check their schedules before confirming.

Rescheduling

Rescheduling is allowed depending on the artist's availability. If you need to change your appointment, inform Arte and the artist as early as possible so adjustments can be made smoothly. Rescheduling fees or conditions, if any, vary per artist and will be communicated upfront.

Rush Bookings

Bookings made with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to a rush fee to cover expedited coordination and scheduling.

  • If Arte matches you with an artist from our current partner network: PHP 250.00 rush fee.
  • If Arte specifically sources and secures an artist for you (e.g., for a unique date or location): PHP 500.00 rush fee.
  • You will only be charged the rush fee once Arte confirms that an artist is able to accommodate your booking. Availability is not guaranteed for rush requests.
  • Artists may also apply their own additional rush fees, which will be disclosed before confirmation.

Lateness

Late fees or policies — for both clients and artists — vary per artist and will be disclosed before booking confirmation.

No-Shows

No-show policies vary per artist. In the event of a client no-show, the non-refundable downpayment is forfeited. If the artist is the one who doesn't show up, see the Artist Accountability section below.

Early Call Time Fee

If your appointment requires the artist to report very early (e.g., before typical working hours), an early call time fee may apply. This accounts for adjusted rest hours, travel readiness, and preparation time. Fees vary per artist and are communicated before booking.

Artist Accountability

Artist-Initiated Cancellations

If an artist needs to cancel, they must notify both Arte and the client immediately and provide a valid reason. The artist is responsible for finding a replacement of equal or better caliber. If they cannot, Arte will step in to assist — and the full downpayment will be returned to the client or transferred to the replacement artist.

Lateness (Artist)

  • Artists must plan their travel and preparation time carefully.
  • If an artist is more than 30 minutes late without proper notice, or if the delay was avoidable, 50% of the downpayment will be forfeited by the artist.
  • If an artist is 1 hour or more late, the client has the right to request a replacement through Arte. In this case, the artist forfeits the full downpayment, which is returned to the client or transferred to the replacement.

Repeated Lateness

Artists who are late 3 or more times without a valid emergency will face the following consequences:

  • Suspended from taking new clients for 1 month.
  • Removal from the 50/50 or full-downpayment-before-service setup.

Exceptions are made for genuine emergencies such as sudden illness or accidents.

Double Bookings

Double booking is strictly prohibited. Taking on another client in the same confirmed time slot — or making unapproved schedule changes that cause delays or complaints — is treated as a double booking and carries serious consequences:

  • If services were rendered: at least 50% of the service fee must be refunded to the affected client.
  • If no services were rendered: a full 100% refund of the downpayment is required.
  • A penalty of PHP 1,000.00–15,000.00 will be charged to Arte for reputational damage.
  • A verified double booking will result in immediate partnership termination.

Any change to a confirmed call time must be reported to Arte immediately. Late arrivals caused by unreported changes carry a PHP 1,000.00 penalty.
