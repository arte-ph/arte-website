Got Questions?

FAQs

How to book with Arte?
Browse artists, choose your service, pick a time slot, add your pegs or requests, and confirm. After the booking is confirmed, the artist will contact you to finalize the details.
Does Arte have fees?
Arte Access applies per booking: ₱250 for bookings below ₱5,000, or 5% for bookings ₱5,000 and above. This covers artist matching, coordination, and booking support, including last-minute changes.
What areas does Arte serve?
Arte primarily operates across Metro Manila and nearby areas in CALABARZON, but we’re not limited to these locations. If you’re outside these areas, let us know and we’ll do our best to assist you.
Is home service available at Arte?
Yes, home service is available! When booking, you can choose between Walk-In (going to the artist's studio) or Home Service (that artist will come to you). Home service requires you to provide your address, and travel fees may apply.
How do I know Arte's artists are legit?
All artists on Arte go through a verification and evaluation process.
How does payment work?
A downpayment is collected through Arte at the time of booking to secure your slot. The remaining balance is settled directly with the artist after the session. Arte holds the downpayment until the session is completed.
How do I join Arte as an artist?
Click “Apply Now,” send us a DM, and submit your details. Our team will review your application, and once approved, we’ll set up your portfolio and connect you with clients.
