At Arte, your privacy matters as much as your glow-up. This Privacy Policy explains how Arte ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform to discover and book beauty professionals — including hair and makeup artists (HMUAs), nail technicians, and lash technicians across the Philippines.

By using Arte, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have questions, our team is always here to help.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information to help you find and book the right beauty professional, and to help our artists grow their business. This includes:

  • Account information — your name, contact number, and email address when you register or inquire through Arte.
  • Booking details — service preferences, appointment date and time, location (for home service), and any pegs or special requests you share.
  • Payment information — downpayment transaction records processed through Arte. We do not store full card details; payments are handled through secure third-party processors.
  • Device and usage data — browser type, pages visited, time spent on the platform, and referring URLs, collected automatically to improve your experience.
  • Communications — messages you send to Arte's social media accounts or through our inquiry forms.
02

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to deliver and improve the Arte experience. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Facilitate your bookings and connect you with verified beauty professionals.
  • Process downpayments and send booking confirmations.
  • Communicate with you about your appointments, updates, and service changes.
  • Verify and onboard artist partners onto the platform.
  • Improve platform features, detect issues, and personalize your experience.
  • Send you relevant updates, promotions, or new features — you can opt out anytime.
  • Comply with applicable laws and resolve any disputes.
03

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your booked artist — we share your name, contact details, location (if home service), and booking preferences so the artist can prepare and reach out to you.
  • With payment processors — to securely handle downpayment transactions.
  • With service providers — third parties that help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, analytics), who are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • For legal compliance — if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.
💅 We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers or advertisers.
04

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Booking records may be kept for up to 3 years for operational and legal purposes.

If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for the resolution of pending disputes.

05

Cookies and Tracking

Arte uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the platform running smoothly and to understand how visitors use our site. These may include:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for core platform functions like session management.
  • Analytics cookies — to understand traffic patterns and improve the platform.
  • Preference cookies — to remember your settings and preferences across visits.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.

06

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You can also:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any email we send.
  • Update your account details by contacting our support team.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, reach out to us at arte.team.ph@gmail.com or via our Facebook page. We'll respond within 7 business days.

07

Security of Your Data

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include secure connections (HTTPS), access controls, and working only with trusted service providers.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we do our best to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong passwords and to notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized use of your account.

08

Children's Privacy

Arte is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe a minor has provided us with their personal information, please contact us at arte.team.ph@gmail.com.

09

Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external websites or social media profiles (such as our Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok pages). This Privacy Policy applies only to Arte's own platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites and encourage you to review their policies before sharing any personal information.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or platform features. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Arte after any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
